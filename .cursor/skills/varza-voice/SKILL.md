# EJAM voice

EJAM gets people off the couch to play more, socialize, and meet someone new. It is a rainy Saturday, you are bored at home, you open EJAM, see what is on nearby, and go. Padel, tennis, bowling, climbing, anything.

Write about people and the plan, not the product. Warm, social, a bit cheeky. Second person. Plain English. Short.

Full visual rules live in `.cursor/rules/varza-design.mdc`. This skill is the copy.

## Positioning

Say this:

- You are bored. There is something on near you. Go.
- Playing is how you meet people.
- The weekend can have a plan in one tap.

Do not say this:

- Startup tool, scheduler, "platform features", unlock, seamless, leverage, supercharge.
- "About ten friends", "small crew", "the crew", "group chat", "instead of WhatsApp".
- WhatsApp anywhere on the homepage or other marketing. The share button inside a game may still say "Share on WhatsApp".
- Feature lists as the headline. Name the feeling and the next step.

Spirit lines, already approved:

- "Bored at home? Go play."
- "Less scrolling, more playing."
- "Your weekend just got a plan."

No exclamation marks. Cheeky is the line, not the punctuation. No "crush it", "let's go", or slang piles.

## Words

Use: play, on near you, this weekend, I'm in, see what's on, start a game, spot, beginners, indoors, outdoors, someone new, show up.

Avoid: crew, squad, gang, epic, awesome, super, simply, game-changer, community platform, users (say people or players). "Just" is fine inside an approved line such as "Your weekend just got a plan." Do not sprinkle it as filler.

Buttons are verbs. Preferred:

| Job | Say | Not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Join | I'm in | Going, RSVP, Attend, Submit |
| Browse | See what's on | Browse events, Explore, Public games |
| Create | Start a game | Plan a game, Create event, New |
| Auth | Log in, Create an account | Sign in, Register, Get started |
| Save | Save | Submit, Update, Confirm changes |
| Waitlist | Join the waitlist | Add me to the queue |

One primary button per screen. Its label is the next step, not a category.

## How to write

Headlines: one short line. A situation, then the move. Question or statement. No subtitle that repeats it.

Empty states: what is true, then the one thing to do. Include the action.

Errors: what went wrong in human words, then the fix. No status codes, no "invalid", no blame.

Emails and notices: one result, one link. Subject is the news. Body is two lines plus a button.

Share messages and link previews: the activity, when, where, and the link. Sound like a person texting a friend, not a press release. Private games include the invite link. Public games use the public page. No WhatsApp in the preview title.

Locked strings, until the copy pass updates the tests in the same change: "Your next game", "Open near you", "Vote on a time", "Availability", "Best time", "Your RSVP", "Lock in this time", "This time has passed".

## Before and after

Headlines:

- Before: "Get the crew together. Pick a time, lock the pitch, play." After: "Bored at home? Go play."
- Before: "What is coming up, and what the rest of the crew has made public." After: "See what's on near you."
- Before: "Organise football with your group chat." After: "Less scrolling, more playing."
- Before: "The weekend, sorted." After: "Your weekend just got a plan."

Buttons and links:

- Before: "Plan a game". After: "Start a game".
- Before: "Going". After: "I'm in".
- Before: "See public games". After: "See what's on".
- Before: "Copy invite link". After: "Copy link".

Empty states:

- Before: "No games yet. Plan one for the crew, or open an invite from the group chat." After: "Nothing on yet. Start a game, or see what's on nearby."
- Before: "Nothing public right now. When a friend plans a public game, it shows up here." After: "Quiet around here. Start something, or check back this weekend."
- Before: "No comments yet. Leave a note for the crew." After: "No notes yet. Say if you're bringing a ball."
- Before: "No games match. Try another sport." After: "Nothing for that. Try another activity, or a wider day."

Helpers:

- Before: "This name is what the crew sees on a game." After: "This is the name people see when you show up."
- Before: "Paste this into WhatsApp. Anyone with the link can open the game." After, inside a game: "Send this to someone who'd be up for it." The button may still be "Share on WhatsApp".
- Before: "Friends are voting on a time." After: "People are still picking a time."

Notices and emails:

- Before: "You are on the list." After: "You're in."
- Before: "You are on the waitlist." After: "You're on the waitlist. We'll move you up if a spot opens."
- Before: "A spot opened. You are going to Thursday five-a-side." After: "You're in. A spot opened for Thursday five-a-side."
- Before, subject: "A spot opened on Thursday five-a-side." After: "You're in for Thursday five-a-side."
- Before, greeting: "You are in." After: "A spot opened."
- Before: "Same time next week is ready. Change anything, then save." After: "Next week is ready. Change anything, then save."

Errors:

- Before: "This game is full. You can still say Maybe or Not going." After: "It's full. Join the waitlist, or say maybe."
- Before: "Pick a time before you mark Going, Maybe, or Not going." After: "Pick a time first. Then say you're in."
- Before: "The max participants field must be at least 2." After: "A game needs at least two spots."
- Before: "Too many tries. Wait a minute, then try again." After: "Too many tries. Wait a minute, then try again." (already plain; keep it)

Share and previews:

- Before: "Thursday five-a-side. Football, Thu 10 Dec, 18:00, Park pitch. {url}" After: "Thursday five-a-side, this Thursday at 18:00, Park pitch. Come play. {url}"
- Before, OG description: "Get the crew together. Pick a time, lock the pitch, play." After: "Bored at home? See what's on near you and go play."
- Before, private preview: "You're invited to a game on EJAM. Log in to see the sport, the time, and the place." After: "Someone wants you in a game. Log in to see when and where."

## Checklist

Run this before shipping any user-facing text:

1. Would a bored person on a Saturday understand it in one read?
2. Is it about playing and people, not about the app?
3. No "crew", "ten friends", "small", "WhatsApp" on marketing pages?
4. In-game share may say WhatsApp. Homepage and previews must not.
5. Button is a verb from the table, and there is only one primary.
6. Empty state says what to do next.
7. Error says the fix, not the validation rule.
8. Email or notice is one result and one link.
9. No exclamation marks. No startup words.
10. Locked test strings are unchanged, or the tests change in the same edit.
